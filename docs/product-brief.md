# Mika Logan Studios Practice Growth Platform

## Product Thesis
A modular client-acquisition and onboarding platform for wellness practices, designed to help solo practitioners convert website visitors into structured, qualified inquiries and route them into the next step.

## First Vertical
Solo therapists / wellness practitioners.

## MVP User Flow
1. Visitor lands on practice website.
2. Visitor clicks “Start a consult request.”
3. Visitor completes guided inquiry form.
4. System saves structured inquiry.
5. Practitioner receives notification.
6. Practitioner views inquiry in dashboard.
7. Practitioner updates lead status.

## Core Modules
- Marketing Website
- Guided Inquiry Flow
- Inquiry Management Dashboard
- Analytics/Event Tracking
- Notification System
- Practice Management Handoff

## Non-Goals
- Not an EHR
- Not clinical documentation
- Not secure messaging
- Not billing
- Not replacing Jane/Owl/SimplePractice
- Not claiming healthcare compliance yet
